# Haven

Plans with people you trust. Haven is a calendar and notes app that keeps your day yours and lets you make plans with the people you keep close. It is in a private alpha with an invited inner team.

If you link a Google calendar, Haven keeps that one calendar and your commitments in step, both ways. The sync runs on your phone: events and sign-in tokens stay there, and our server never sees calendar data. See the privacy policy for the details, including how Haven follows Google's Limited Use requirements.
